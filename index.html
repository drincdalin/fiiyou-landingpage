A small, open source social network.

Make your profile look the way you want, find the people around you, and chat with friends.

    Make your page look the way you want, from the colours to the layout.

  • Share music and photos

    See who is around you and say hi.

  • Chat with friends

    Talk to the people you found, right inside the app.

No ads, no tracking.

Help keep it running

fiiyou has no ads, so it runs on donations. The money goes to keeping the database and the service online, and to building new features.

We prefer a bank transfer, because Ko-fi takes a percentage off every donation and a transfer lets the whole amount reach fiiyou. Ko-fi works too if that's easier for you.

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. fiiyou has no ads and nothing is locked behind a payment. It runs on donations.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It opens in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen. A computer gives you more room.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. You can export it or delete it whenever you want.

Are messages private?

Chat is not end to end encrypted, so the person running fiiyou could technically read messages. The privacy policy has the details.

Can I help build it?

Yes. The code is public on GitHub. Send a pull request and you land on the contributors list above.
